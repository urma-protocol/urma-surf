## Embedded fonts

Adwaita Sans and Adwaita Mono are bundled unmodified, under SIL OFL 1.1.
The full copyright and license text supplied with the recorded
adwaita-fonts package is retained verbatim in `fonts/LICENSE`.
`fonts/README.md` records the source, exact file hashes and runtime use.
The embedded TTF metadata also retains its original copyright and OFL
references. The Mono metadata uses the range 2015–2026, while the supplied
package notice says 2015–2025; both are preserved without editing either
upstream artifact. That observation is not a finding that the fonts are
unlicensed.

Installed copies include this notice, `LICENSE`, `fonts/LICENSE` and the
asset provenance under `share/doc/urma-browser/`.
